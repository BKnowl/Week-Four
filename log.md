## Fourth Week Log 
___
#### Session at the Billings Estate 
- The fourth week was spent at the Billings Estate in the Old Ottawa South neighbourhood. When we arrived we were escorted to the gravesite where the guide told us up to 150 people had been buried. Through time their inital gravestones were graffitied and vandalized. Thus around 2010 they began erecting new, more modern looking stones to replace those before them.
- I found the experience overall to be pretty fun. I had heard about the Billings Estate before but never really knew where it was, I always imagined it'd have to be near Billings Bridge but never expected it to be in that of an inconspicuous spot.
___
#### Successes 
- Honestly the session was very rewarding. I really enjoyed being able to use the Lidar machine, it gave me a view of the grave in a way my eyes probably ever could. It allowed me to really see its dimensions and depth in respect to the topology.
- Secondly I really really enjoyed using Polycam. I liked it because I was in control of what I scanned. In respect to the lasar scanner that Kavita was showing, Polycam was less rigid, it provided more functionality. I couldn't imagine having to use the big lasar scanner in that garage space where the original gravestones were kept. I don't think it would've given me the same freedom in that environment that Polycam provided. I ended up scanned a few of the stones inside the garage, the whole garage itself, a few of the small artefacts kept outisde on the picnic benches, and finally before I left I scanned the entirety of the old Smokehouse. I kind of loved the scanned aspect, and seeing it upload into being a pretty precise digital replica. I can start to see the importance and value that a scanner service like this can have. 
___
#### Failures
- For failures I genuinely don't think there really is one this week because, maybe similarly to last week, it was more about putting yourself out there and trying something new. I had never done Lidar or worked with Lasar scanners and Polycam so it was all really new to me, and even though I may have moved a little to quickly a few times with the lasar scanner or missed a segment on polycam, i'm not hard on myself for that because I adjusted quickly and got back on track.
___
#### Overall
- This week was pretty redeeming i'd say. Being able to have class at a heritage site and do some pretty cool stuff with some tech that is new to me was awesome.
- The biggest takeaway for me was my enthusiasm and liking (which I didn't expect to happen) to Polycam. I liked being in control, I liked being able to scan really anything, and I think the 3D digital copy is so cool. It's definitely the type of thing I want to continue using.
